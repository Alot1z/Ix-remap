8 · How it actually works (the 6 steps that buy the sharpness)

  1. Decode by hand, zero deps. PNG chunks → zlib.inflateSync → unfilter (types 0–4, Paeth included). The 5400×5440 asset decodes in ~1s, no image library in the dependency tree.
  2. Tight-crop to the ink bounding box. The full canvas wastes 21% of its rows on empty navy; cropping spends every cell of the grid on actual art — free resolution.
  3. Coverage-thresholded supersampling. Per target cell: ink_frac = fraction of source pixels that are ink; the cell is ink iff ink_frac ≥ 0.5. The shape boundary lands exactly on the 50% coverage contour — hard edges, no halo, and averaging now helps (it positions edges sub-pixel accurately) instead of hurting.
  4. Ink-only color mean. Edge cells average only their ink pixels, never blending background navy into the tone — this kills the dark mud halo that made the old render look out-of-focus.
  5. Flat 5-tone snap. Luminance is normalized to the asset's ink range, then quantized to 5 flat brand tones at fixed boundaries (49 · 85.5 · 133.5 · 198). The gradient becomes deliberate pixel-art bands — exactly the skills.sh "sharp" read.
  6. Half-block emit with run-length SGR. Two pixel rows per char cell (▀ / ▄ / space), colors change only when they actually change (352 escapes for the whole 80-col banner), every line ends in reset. 256 mode pre-snaps the same 5 tones into the xterm cube; ASCII maps them to . - = * #.
